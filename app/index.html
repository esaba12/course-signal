METHOD & LIMITS

What this view knows—and what it does not.

Historical values use the configured institution source and compare like-for-like academic terms. The estimate selects the strongest of three simple methods using historical backtests.

Source: Loading source…

It can show

  • Comparable-term history from the configured source
  • A transparent estimate and historic error
  • An optional verified current-status snapshot

It cannot show

  • Unprovided capacity, fill rate, or waitlist size
  • Local staffing, rooms, or curriculum constraints
  • Individual student intent or an automatic decision
