METHOD & LIMITS

What this view knows—and what it does not.

Historical values aggregate public records of students receiving a final grade, compared like-for-like by calendar term. The estimate selects the strongest of three simple methods using historical backtests.

It can show

  • Comparable-term completed-grade history
  • A transparent estimate and historic error
  • A verified cached Open/Closed snapshot, when available

It cannot show

  • Seats, fill rate, or waitlist size
  • Staffing, rooms, or curriculum constraints
  • Individual student intent or a decision to add/cut a class
