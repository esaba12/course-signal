What this actually measures

Historical values are aggregated public records of students receiving a final grade in a CS course, compared like-for-like by calendar term. The estimate selects the best of three deliberately simple methods using historic backtests. It does not know seats, capacity, waitlists, rooms, staffing, or individual student intent.
