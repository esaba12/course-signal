# Brag Plan: Course Signal

## The 9-question rubric
1. **What is the app?** An institution-configurable, advisor-facing course-planning signal built from historical enrollment data — UIUC is the live demo adapter, not the product scope.
2. **Funniest/most impressive claim:** The product refuses to overclaim — its own docs ban phrases like "we predict capacity" or "we know demand." The restraint itself is the credibility.
3. **Visual hook:** The real 3-card summary grid — Recent Observed Change / Next Comparable Term (the primary card, orange top border) / Model Check — landing over the readiness strip's green "Historical signal ready" dot.
4. **What to show:** The real hero line, the real institution-neutral organization picker (UI card + "Demo" badge), and the real dashboard grid with CS 225 as the worked example (the project's own pitch-script demo course).
5. **Shortest satisfying video:** ~18-19s.
6. **Tone:** preset `polished`; direction: "restrained, guardrail-conscious confidence — precision over hype, matching the product's own explicit 'do not say' list."
7. **Audio:** `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (steady, clean — matches `polished`/`cinematic`). Beat-lock the primary "Next Comparable Term" card's number landing to the strong cue at 12.55s.
8. **Share caption:** "Course Signal turns historical enrollment data into an advisor-facing planning signal — configurable per institution, with UIUC as the live demo. It refuses to fake a capacity forecast, and says so."
9. **User flow worth showing:** entry (organization picker → UIUC demo) → key action (CS 225 comparison, Fall vs. Fall) → result (the 3-card grid: observed change, estimate, backtest).

## The angle
Everything in the video is drawn straight from the product's own restraint: no invented stats, no "AI-powered" hype language, just the real hero line, the real institution-neutral picker, and the real 3-card grid with its actual labels. The close borrows the same discipline the project's own pitch script insists on.
